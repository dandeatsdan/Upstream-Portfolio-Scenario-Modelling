# Research and Requirements

The [Research and Requirements evidence pack](research_and_requirements.pdf) examines the reliance on manual spreadsheet transformations and specialist knowledge in Upstream portfolio planning. It sets out the case for a governed scenario modelling framework that balances flexibility, usability, transparency and reproducibility.

| PDF pages | Content |
| --- | --- |
| 2–6 | Research context, ethical problem framing, bias and fairness, and the compliance and security context. |
| 7–11 | Literature review, research gap, objectives and the case-study methodology. |
| 12–13 | Requirements elicitation and an overview of the 22 stakeholder requirements. |
| 14–25 | Supporting analysis, confidentiality considerations and literature synthesis in Appendices A–E. |
| 26–29 | Stakeholder requirements register, including design purpose and priority, in Appendix F. |

See [Solution Architecture](../02_architecture/README.md) for the implemented design and [Testing and Validation](../03_testing_and_validation/README.md) for the evaluation evidence.
